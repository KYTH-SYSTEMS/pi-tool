New: one-tap remote access. On the Tailscale card (when connected) there's now "Remote access: open phone VPN" — it sets your Pi's tailnet IP (100.x) as the host and opens the Tailscale app so you can turn on the VPN there. Then "Connect" and you're on the Pi from anywhere. (For Android reasons the app can't toggle the phone VPN itself — it just opens the Tailscale app; if it's not installed, the Play Store opens.)
