Pi-Tool manages your Raspberry Pi over SSH — now a cockpit with three tabs (Services, Automation, Terminal). Detect, install and update services (evcc, Pi-hole, Home Assistant, Grafana and more) — plus the whole system. Remote access (Raspberry Pi Connect, Tailscale), scheduled updates + health alerts (systemd timers on the Pi), backups, console, file browser, config editor, multiple profiles, biometric lock, SSH key. Unofficial, not affiliated with evcc/Pi-hole.
