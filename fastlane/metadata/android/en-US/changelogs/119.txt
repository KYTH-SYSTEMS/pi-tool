Bug fix: "Restart DNS" on the Pi-hole card had no effect on Pi-hole v6 – yet the app reported success.

The reason: v6 no longer knows the command the app used, and it answers unknown commands with its help text and a success code. The app now asks the installed Pi-hole which command it understands, and reports a genuine failure as one. The same applied to the DNS reload after restoring a Pi-hole backup.
