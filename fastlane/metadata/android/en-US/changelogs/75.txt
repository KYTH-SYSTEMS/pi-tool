Pi-Tool – a cockpit for your Raspberry Pi: detect, install and update self-hosted services (evcc, Pi-hole, Home Assistant, Grafana and more) over SSH – plus the whole system. Remote access (Raspberry Pi Connect, Tailscale), file browser, backups, console, config editor, scheduled updates + health alerts as on-Pi systemd timers, multiple profiles, biometric lock, SSH key. Unofficial, not affiliated with evcc/Pi-hole.
